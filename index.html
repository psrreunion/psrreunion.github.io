    Tell families where to stay. Families book and pay for their own rooms.

    Step 4 of 10

    Ages and prices

    Set the age range and price for each group. Click any box to change it.

    Put 0 for anyone who comes free. If you don’t want early-bird pricing, type the same price in both columns.

    Want to see how it looks? When you’ve finished filling in this page, click the button below. It shows you what families will see. This is only a look. Nothing is sent to anyone, and you can come right back here.

    Step 5 of 10

    ExtrasOptional

    Choose the items families can add when they register, such as t-shirts or boxed lunches.

    Check the box to show an item on the registration form. Uncheck it to hide the item without deleting it.

    Families will be asked if anyone is vegetarian, vegan, or doesn’t eat pork. This helps you plan meals so everyone has something to eat.

    Want to see how it looks? When you’ve finished filling in this page, click the button below. It shows you what families will see. This is only a look. Nothing is sent to anyone, and you can come right back here.

    Step 6 of 10

    Emails

    Emails to families go out for you automatically. Pick the email where you want families’ replies and any alerts to come. Then choose how your emails look.

    When families reply, it comes to this email. We’ll also email you here if something looks wrong, such as:
    • A payment we can’t match
    • A double payment
    • An email that didn’t send
    Every reply is also saved on the Records page.
    Step 7 of 10

    Money you already have

    Enter how much is already in the reunion account and the scholarship fund.

    Starting balances count as $0 in Test mode, so they’re hidden here. Turn Test mode off to see or change them.

    If you’re not sure, leave it blank. You can change these numbers later.

    Where you’ll keep track of money

    After setup, these pages at the top keep track of the money for you.

    Payments: money families pay you
    Expenses: what you spend. Add a receipt when you have one, so the records are complete for the next host and the board.
    Ledger: all money in and out, your balance and the report
    Step 8 of 10

    Costs and budget

    Enter what your venue or caterer charges, then plan what you expect to spend.

    If something doesn’t apply to your reunion, leave it blank. Registration fees are based on the total cost of the reunion, so the more people who register, the less each person pays.

    Planned budget

    This is what you plan to spend on each item, not money already spent. Catering and the deposit fill in for you from the boxes above.

    Step 9 of 10

    Check-in helpers

    Helpers use a link to check families in at the reunion. There’s nothing to type on this step.

    Click Copy helper link and text it to your helpers. They can open it on a phone, tablet or laptop. Helpers can’t see money or the hosting committee pages. A new link is made each year, and it stops working the day after the reunion.

    Step 10 of 10

    Open registration

    This is the last step. Choose when families can register.

    What’s next

    The tabs at the top of the page do the rest:

    • Home: your shortcuts.
    • Register: sign up a family yourself, like walk-ins or someone without internet.
    • Overview: everything at a glance, like registrations, payments, budget, and more.
    • Payments: see who’s paid and who still owes. Add cash, check, or committee payments, send reminders to anyone who hasn’t paid in full, and resend receipts.
    • Expenses: add and see everything you’ve spent, with or without receipts.
    • Ledger: every dollar in and out, like a checkbook. Print the full report here. Any receipts you’ve uploaded are added to it automatically.
    • Check-in: see who has arrived and send the check-in link to your helpers.
    • Records: your contact list and every email sent and received. You can download both.
