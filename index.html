    Set up your reunion one step at a time. Click Save and continue at the bottom of each step. You can come back and change anything later. Page version: Nov 17, 2026

    All changes saved

    Click any step to go straight to it.

    Step 1 of 10

    About your reunion

    Tell families about this year’s reunion: the family reunion name, the number, the theme and the dates.

    These words show at the top of the registration page and in every email. You can change them at any time.

    Step 2 of 10

    Weekend schedule and form choices

    Show families what is happening each day of the reunion. The schedule is optional.

    Type the day on one line, then each event on its own line under it. Click See example to see how it looks. If you leave this blank, families won’t see a schedule.

    Step 3 of 10

    Hotel

    Tell families where to stay. Families book and pay for their own rooms.

    The booking link puts a Book your room button on the registration page. Until you add it, the button won’t show.

    Step 4 of 10

    Ages and prices

    Set the age range and price for each group. Click any box to change it.

    Put 0 for anyone who comes free. If you don’t want early-bird pricing, type the same price in both columns.

    Step 5 of 10

    ExtrasOptional

    Choose the items families can add when they register, such as t-shirts or boxed lunches.

    Check the box to show an item on the registration form. Uncheck it to hide the item without deleting it.

    Step 6 of 10

    Emails

    Choose how emails to families look and where their replies go.

    When a family replies to one of your emails, the reply goes to the reunion email first. It’s saved on the Emails tab of the sheet, then sent on to this address. The reunion name, dates and hotel are added to every email for you.

    Who else should get alerts (optional)

    We’ll email you if something looks wrong, such as a payment we couldn’t match or an email that didn’t send.

    Reunion committee email

    After the reunion, when the next host signs up, we email the reunion committee here so they know who it is. Use the committee’s shared email if it has one. If this email is ever changed, the old address gets a notice.

    Step 7 of 10

    Money you already have

    Enter how much is already in the reunion account and the scholarship fund.

    Starting balances count as $0 in Test mode, so they’re hidden here. Turn Test mode off to see or change them.

    If you’re not sure, put 0. You can change these numbers later.

    Step 8 of 10

    Costs and budget

    Enter what your venue or caterer charges, then plan what you expect to spend.

    If something doesn’t apply to your reunion, put 0. Registration fees are based on the total cost of the reunion, so the more people who register, the less each person pays.

    Planned budget

    This is what you plan to spend on each item, not money already spent. Catering and the deposit fill in for you from the boxes above.

    Step 9 of 10

    Check-in helpers

    Helpers use this code to check families in at the reunion. There’s nothing to type on this step.

    Click Copy helper link and text it to your helpers. They can open it on a phone, tablet or laptop and type the code to check people in. Helpers can’t see money or the hosting committee pages. A new code is made each year, and it stops working the day after the reunion.

    Step 10 of 10

    Open registration

    This is the last step. Add your payment link and choose when families can register.

    Until this is filled in, families see “Online payment opens soon” after they register.

    Registration opens by itself on the date you pick. It stays open through the last day of the reunion, so people can still sign up at the door.

    Switch to Open when you’re ready, or let the date above do it. When this is Closed, families see “Registration opens soon.”

    Test mode lets the hosting committee practice without touching real records. Anything entered goes to the red Test tabs in the sheet. Turn this on to show the Test mode button on the home screen, or off to hide it. When this is off, everything you enter goes into the real reunion records.

    You’re all set

    Everything is saved. Click Preview registration page to see exactly what families will see.

    Need to change something later?

    Click Setup at the top of the page. Then click the step you need in the list on the left, such as Hotel or Ages and prices. You don’t have to go through every step again.
